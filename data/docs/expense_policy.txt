MERIDIAN ANALYTICS GROUP - EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
Document ID: FIN-315
Effective date: 2023-07-01
Last revised: 2025-01-10
Owner: Finance

1. GENERAL PRINCIPLES
---------------------
Employees are reimbursed for reasonable, necessary business expenses incurred
in the course of their work. All expenses must be submitted within 60 days of
the expense date. Claims older than 60 days require Finance Director approval,
and claims older than 120 days will be rejected outright with no exceptions.

2. TRAVEL
---------
- Airfare: economy class for flights under 6 hours; premium economy is allowed
  for flights of 6 hours or longer. Business class requires Director approval
  for flights over 10 hours.
- Ground transportation: ride-share or taxi is reimbursable; personal mileage is
  reimbursed at the IRS standard rate ($0.67/mile in 2024, $0.70/mile in 2025).
- Lodging: up to $250/night in Tier 1 cities (New York, San Francisco, London,
  Singapore) and up to $150/night elsewhere.
- Meals while traveling: up to $75/day domestic, $100/day international.

3. MEALS AND ENTERTAINMENT
--------------------------
- Client meals: reimbursable with itemized receipt and a list of attendees.
- Team meals: reimbursable up to $40/person when tied to a business purpose.
- Alcohol: NOT reimbursable except at approved client entertainment events,
  capped at $30/person.
- Solo meals during regular work days at the employee's home city are NOT
  reimbursable.

4. HOME OFFICE
--------------
- The one-time home office stipend ($500) is administered by People Operations,
  not Finance, and is therefore NOT submitted through the expense system.
- Ergonomic equipment (chairs, standing desks) may be expensed up to $300 per
  fiscal year with manager pre-approval. A receipt is mandatory.

5. SOFTWARE AND SUBSCRIPTIONS
------------------------------
- Individual software purchases under $100/month may be expensed with manager
  approval.
- Subscriptions over $100/month require a procurement review and a completed
  security assessment (see IT-SEC-101, section 5).

6. APPROVAL THRESHOLDS
-----------------------
- Under $500: direct manager approval.
- $500-$2,500: manager + department head approval.
- Over $2,500: manager + department head + Finance Director approval.

7. RECEIPTS
------------
Itemized receipts are required for any single expense of $25 or more. For
expenses under $25, a credit-card statement line is sufficient.

8. CORPORATE CREDIT CARD
-------------------------
Corporate cards are issued to employees who travel at least 4 times per year.
Personal use of the corporate card is prohibited. Corporate card transactions
must be reconciled monthly in the expense system.

NOTE ON POLICY CHANGES: The mileage reimbursement rate is updated annually to
match the IRS standard rate. The 2024 rate ($0.67) applied to expenses incurred
in 2024; the 2025 rate ($0.70) applies to expenses incurred on or after
2025-01-01. Do not retroactively apply a new rate to prior-year expenses.
