MERIDIAN ANALYTICS GROUP - REMOTE WORK POLICY
Document ID: HR-204
Effective date: 2024-01-01
Last revised: 2025-03-15
Owner: People Operations

1. ELIGIBILITY
---------------
Full-time employees who have completed their 90-day probation period are
eligible for remote work. Contractors and interns are not eligible for the
formal remote-work program, though managers may approve ad-hoc remote days at
their discretion.

2. WORK ARRANGEMENT TYPES
-------------------------
- Hybrid (default): 2-3 days per week on-site, remainder remote. Most roles are
  hired into this arrangement.
- Fully Remote: requires VP approval and is reserved for roles where on-site
  presence is demonstrably unnecessary (e.g., certain engineering and support
  positions).
- Temporary Remote: up to 8 weeks per calendar year, for personal or medical
  reasons, approved by the direct manager.

3. CORE HOURS
--------------
Regardless of arrangement, all employees must be available during core hours
of 10:00-16:00 local time. Outside core hours, schedules are flexible provided
the employee works 40 hours per week on average.

EXCEPTION: Employees in the Customer Support organization are exempt from core
hours and instead follow their assigned shift schedule, because support is
staffed across time zones.

4. WORK LOCATION REQUIREMENTS
------------------------------
Remote employees must work from a location within the country of their
employment contract. Working from another country requires a written
cross-border authorization from Legal and HR; unauthorized overseas work may
create tax liability and is grounds for disciplinary action.

Effective 2025-04-01, employees may work from any location within their country
without notifying their manager. Previously (before 2025-04-01), a notification
to the manager was required for any remote work outside the employee's home
city. Employees who moved before this change should not be penalized for having
followed the older notification rule.

5. EQUIPMENT AND STIPEND
------------------------
- Company-issued laptop: provided to all remote and hybrid employees.
- Home office stipend: one-time $500 for fully remote employees hired after
  2023-01-01. Hybrid employees are NOT eligible for the stipend.
- Monthly internet stipend: $50/month for fully remote employees only.
- Employees are responsible for a reliable internet connection of at least
  25 Mbps downstream.

6. SECURITY REQUIREMENTS FOR REMOTE WORK
----------------------------------------
- Company devices must use full-disk encryption (see IT-SEC-101).
- Public Wi-Fi use requires the company VPN at all times.
- Confidential documents must never be printed at home or stored on personal
  devices.

7. APPROVAL AND REVIEW
-----------------------
Remote arrangements are reviewed annually. A manager may revoke a remote
arrangement with 30 days' written notice if business needs change. Employees
who wish to appeal a revocation may escalate to their department head, and then
to People Operations; the appeal must be filed within 14 days of the notice.
