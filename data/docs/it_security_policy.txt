MERIDIAN ANALYTICS GROUP - IT SECURITY AND PASSWORD POLICY
Document ID: IT-SEC-101
Effective date: 2024-06-01
Last revised: 2025-02-20
Owner: Information Security

1. SCOPE
--------
Applies to all employees, contractors, and interns using company systems,
regardless of work arrangement.

2. PASSWORD REQUIREMENTS
------------------------
- Minimum length: 14 characters.
- Must include at least three of: uppercase, lowercase, digit, symbol.
- Company-wide passwords must be rotated every 180 days.
- Passwords may not be reused within the last 10 password cycles.
- Passphrases (e.g., four random words) are encouraged and satisfy the length
  requirement.

EXCEPTION: Service and machine accounts (non-human accounts) are exempt from
expiration and rotation. They must instead use 32-character randomly generated
secrets stored in the company secrets manager, and are rotated only on offboarding
or suspected compromise.

2b. MULTI-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION
--------------------------------
MFA is mandatory for all company accounts as of 2024-06-01. SMS-based MFA was
permitted until 2024-12-31 but is now prohibited; use an authenticator app or a
hardware security key. Employees still on SMS MFA after 2025-01-01 must
re-enroll immediately.

3. DEVICE SECURITY
------------------
- Full-disk encryption is mandatory on all company devices (BitLocker or
  FileVault).
- Screen lock after 5 minutes of inactivity.
- OS and security patches must be installed within 14 days of release; critical
  patches within 72 hours.
- Personal devices may access email only through the managed mobile app with a
  device passcode; jailbroken or rooted devices are prohibited.

4. NETWORK AND VPN
------------------
- The company VPN is required on any untrusted network (public Wi-Fi, hotels,
  airports).
- Split tunneling is disabled on the corporate VPN.
- Guest Wi-Fi in company offices is isolated from the corporate network.

5. SOFTWARE AND THIRD-PARTY SERVICES
------------------------------------
- Installing unapproved software on company devices is prohibited.
- Any third-party service processing company data requires a security
  assessment before use. Services handling customer PII additionally require a
  signed Data Processing Agreement.
- Approval thresholds: no assessment needed under $0 spend but still requires a
  security review if PII is involved; standard assessment for most tools;
  enhanced assessment for anything touching production data.

6. DATA CLASSIFICATION AND HANDLING
------------------------------------
- Public: may be shared freely.
- Internal: default classification; do not share outside the company without
  approval.
- Confidential: includes customer PII and financial data; encrypted at rest and
  in transit; access on a need-to-know basis.
- Restricted: includes secrets and security keys; never stored in documents,
  email, or chat; only in the secrets manager.

7. INCIDENT REPORTING
----------------------
Any suspected security incident must be reported to security@meridian.example
within 1 hour of discovery. Do NOT attempt to investigate independently. The
security team will triage and may engage legal and communications.

8. ACCESS REVIEW
----------------
Access to Confidential and Restricted data is reviewed quarterly. Access for
departing employees is revoked within 24 hours of the last working day; for
involuntary terminations, revocation is immediate.

9. PASSWORD RESET CHANGES (HISTORY)
------------------------------------
Before 2024-06-01, the minimum password length was 8 characters and rotation was
every 90 days. Those older requirements no longer apply to accounts created or
reset on or after 2024-06-01. Help-desk documentation referencing the old 8/90
rules is outdated and should be disregarded.
